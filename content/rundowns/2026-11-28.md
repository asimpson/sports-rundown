**NFL (Black Friday)**

- **Steelers 27, Broncos 5.** Pittsburgh's first game since releasing Aaron Rodgers was a rout. T.J. Watt and Jalen Ramsey each scored a defensive touchdown, and Mason Rudolph led the offense. The 27-5 final is a first in NFL history (Scorigami), and so is the 24-5 halftime score.

**NBA**

Stephen Curry sat out with an oblique abdominal tear and will be re-evaluated Wednesday. Yaxel Lendeborg led the Warriors with 18 points, De'Anthony Melton had 17 and eight rebounds, and Brandin Podziemski had 16. Deni Avdija led Portland with 26.
