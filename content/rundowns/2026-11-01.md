**College Football: Big Ten**

- **Michigan 28, Rutgers 21.** Freshman QB Tommy Carr threw for 266 yards and a touchdown and ran for two more, including the 9-yard go-ahead score that broke a 21-21 tie with about four minutes left. Michigan put up 427 yards, its most in a Big Ten game this season, and the 28 points are also its high in conference play. Savion Hiter ran for 76 yards, and JJ Buchanan had 63 receiving. Michigan is 5-3 (2-3). **Next: Michigan State at Michigan Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 PM ET on Peacock.** {{< x url="https://x.com/CFBONFOX/status/2116612778819543335" label="Another Tommy Carr rushing touchdown" >}}
- **USC 30, Ohio State 18.** This was the upset of the day. No. 2 Ohio State started freshman QB Luke Fahey (260 passing yards), and USC led 14-3 after one, sparked by a 69-yard Jayden Maiava to Trent Mosley touchdown. Bo Jackson ran for 121 for the Buckeyes. Ohio State falls to 6-2 (4-1), and USC is 7-2 (4-2).
- **Indiana 23, Minnesota 16 (OT).** Indiana switched QBs from Josh Hoover to Grant Wilson and had to answer a 60-yard Minnesota field goal with a 60-yarder of its own to force overtime. The Hoosiers are 8-1 (5-1).
- **Iowa 30, Wisconsin 0.** Hank Brown threw for 222 yards in the rain at Kinnick, and Iowa led 20-0 at the half.
- **Oregon 34, Northwestern 26.** Oregon came back to beat No. 18 Northwestern.
- **Penn State 31, Purdue 17.** It's Matt Campbell's first Big Ten win at Penn State.
- **Illinois 20, Maryland 17.**
- **Washington 17, Nebraska 10.**
- **UCLA 27, Nevada 0.** UCLA is 8-0 and still alone atop the Big Ten at 5-0. Next week brings Oregon at Ohio State (3:30 PM ET, CBS).
