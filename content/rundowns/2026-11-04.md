**College Football: Big Ten**

- **Michigan (5-3, 2-3) is unranked in the first College Football Playoff rankings and hosts Michigan State on Saturday at 7:30 PM ET on Peacock.** Kyle Whittingham says freshman Tommy Carr stays the starter for the foreseeable future after going 22 of 27 for 266 yards at Rutgers, and Michigan has reopened its kicking competition (Trey Butowski, Jacob Baggett and Stuart Blake). Whittingham called the rivalry "the Super Bowl, particularly for them," and says about 55 players on the roster have never played in it. Michigan is a double-digit favorite going for a fifth straight win in the series.
- **First CFP rankings:** Miami is No. 1 for the first time in program history, followed by Georgia, Utah and defending champion Indiana (8-1), which holds the last first-round bye at No. 4. Six Big Ten teams made the top 25: Indiana (4), unbeaten UCLA (8), Ohio State (12), Iowa (19), USC (20) and Oregon (23). **NBA**

- **No games Tuesday.** The league paused for Election Day, and play resumes tonight with 14 games, led by 76ers-Pistons (7 ET) and Mavericks-Spurs (9:30 ET) on ESPN.
- **Warriors: idle Tuesday; next up Heat at Chase Center, Thursday at 10 PM ET.** The fallout from Monday's 116-113 win over Phoenix is all Steph. He scored 25 of his 34 in the second half, including 18 in a 42-point third quarter, right after Draymond Green was ejected for shoving Khaman Maluach and tangling with Dillon Brooks. Curry said Green "lit a fire underneath us." His late miss at the line ended a 51-for-51 start on free throws, two shy of tying his own 52-in-a-row mark from 2017-18. "I'm shocked when I miss anytime," he said. At 38, Curry leads the league in PER and box plus-minus, and Golden State (5-3) has won five straight after an 0-3 start. {{< x url="https://x.com/warriors/status/2117686255807050091" label="The art of the Curry look-away three" >}}
