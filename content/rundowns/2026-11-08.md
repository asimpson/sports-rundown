**College Football: Big Ten**

- **Michigan State 15, Michigan 6.** The Spartans reclaimed the Paul Bunyan Trophy in Ann Arbor for the first time since 2021, as Liam Boyd kicked five field goals and Michigan State forced four turnovers (two interceptions, two fumbles). The Wolverines (5-4, 2-4) managed only 192 yards, converted 2-of-10 third downs, and were outpossessed 38:07 to 21:53; they didn't score until Savion Hiter's 1-yard run with 1:35 left, set up by a 31-yard catch from JJ Buchanan, and the two-point try failed. Tommy Carr was sacked five times. Michigan visits Oregon next Saturday at 3:30 PM ET on FS1. {{< x url="https://x.com/MSU_Football/status/2119291477700255910" label="Spartans reclaim Paul Bunyan" >}} (~56K impressions).

- **No. 23 Oregon 41, No. 12 Ohio State 28.** The Ducks (6-3, 4-2) erased a 14-0 first-quarter deficit with 38 unanswered points at Ohio Stadium. Dylan Raiola threw for 257 yards and two touchdowns, and Dierre Hill Jr. ran for 75 yards and two scores, including a 48-yard burst. Ohio State fell to 6-3 (4-2).

- **No. 5 UCLA 34, Minnesota 20.** The Bruins stayed unbeaten at 9-0 (6-0) as Ty Dieffenbach threw for a career-high 303 yards and three touchdowns, including consecutive 75-yard scoring strikes to Aidan Mizell (150 yards) in the fourth quarter. Wayne Knight ran for 109 yards and a score. Minnesota fell to 5-4 (3-3).

- **No. 19 Iowa 24, Northwestern 10.** Hank Brown threw and ran for touchdowns, L.J. Phillips Jr. rushed for 128 yards, and Braeden Jackson returned a kickoff 96 yards for a score as the Hawkeyes (7-2, 4-2) handed Northwestern (6-3, 3-3) its first loss in the new Ryan Field.

- **Maryland 34, Purdue 29.** Malik Washington threw for 253 yards and two scores and ran for two more as the Terps (3-6, 1-5) poured in 21 third-quarter points for their first Big Ten win and Aazaar Abdul-Rahim's first victory as interim coach. Chris Durr Jr. had 127 yards, including an 87-yard catch, and DeJuan Williams added a 75-yard touchdown run. Purdue fell to 3-6 (2-4).

- **Penn State 28, Washington 25.** Rocco Becht threw for 240 yards and two touchdowns and Carson Hansen ran for a season-high 119 yards and a score as the Nittany Lions (5-4, 2-4) built a 28-6 halftime lead and held on in Seattle. Washington fell to 5-4 (2-4).

- **Wisconsin 31, Rutgers 10.** True freshman Ryan Hopkins, in his first career start, completed 18-of-24 passes for 241 yards and three touchdowns as the Badgers (6-3, 4-2) earned bowl eligibility. Rutgers fell to 2-7 (1-5).

- USC and Indiana were on bye.

**NBA**

- **Warriors 126, Magic 119 (OT).** Stephen Curry scored 41 points (13-of-19 FG, 5-of-10 from three, 10-of-10 FT) with six rebounds and six assists in 35:45 as Golden State (7-3) won its seventh straight, the longest streak since March 2025. Curry poured in 12 points over the final four-plus minutes of regulation, including a go-ahead layup with 25.2 seconds left, before Jamal Cain's putback with 2 seconds remaining sent it to overtime. In OT, Curry converted a three-point play with 1:38 left and added a layup in the final minute. It was his 16th 40-point game since turning 35, an NBA record. Yaxel Lendeborg had 23 points and eight rebounds, Brandin Podziemski scored 15, and Draymond Green dished a season-high 13 assists. Franz Wagner led Orlando with 30 points and 11 assists. {{< x url="https://x.com/NBA/status/2119294799128006973" label="Crunch-time Curry drops 41" >}} (~117K impressions). {{< x url="https://x.com/warriors/status/2119282876222964164" label="Curry's OT and-one" >}} (~115K impressions).

- **Hawks 124, Nets 121.** Jalen Johnson returned and scored 30 points as Atlanta held on in a tight one.

- **Nuggets 114, Pacers 102** in Mexico City. Nikola Jokić had 19 points, 13 rebounds, and eight assists, and Cameron Johnson added 18. Pascal Siakam led Indiana with 23.

- **Knicks 109, Cavaliers 97.** Jalen Brunson scored 31 points with nine assists and Karl-Anthony Towns added 29 points and 13 rebounds. Donovan Mitchell led Cleveland with 37.

- **Thunder 114, Wizards 75.** Shai Gilgeous-Alexander scored 30 points with nine assists in three quarters as Oklahoma City held Washington to 75 points, its fewest since 2017-18, for a fourth straight win.

- **Suns 102, Rockets 99.** Devin Booker scored 24 as Phoenix earned its first back-to-back wins of the season. Kevin Durant had 21 points and 10 rebounds for Houston.

- **Spurs 125, Trail Blazers 103.** Victor Wembanyama scored 29 points and grabbed seven rebounds in three quarters as San Antonio rolled to its eighth straight win.
