**NFL: Thursday Night Football**

- **Packers 27, Panthers 19.** Jordan Love completed 30 of 41 passes for 288 yards and 3 TDs, to Tucker Kraft, Matthew Golden and Josh Jacobs, plus one interception. Jacobs scored twice, once on a 4-yard catch and once on a 1-yard run, though he managed only 39 yards on 21 carries. Green Bay led 27-10 in the 3rd quarter and survived three turnovers. Carolina got within eight on Bryce Young's 26-yard TD pass to Jalen Coker on 4th-and-3 with 5:37 left, but the 2-point try failed. The Panthers then turned it over on downs at midfield. The Packers sacked Young six times, and none of their last five opponents has scored more than 20. Green Bay is 5-3, and Carolina is 3-4. {{< x url="https://x.com/NFL/status/2116008685986287963" label="The final play of Panthers vs. Packers" >}} (~640K views). It's the most-watched clip of the night: Carolina tried a desperation string of laterals, and it ended in a fumble.
- **Christian Watson broke a rib on a 53-yard catch in the 2nd quarter and was taken to the hospital.** A CT scan showed no internal bleeding, so he came back to the game and finished with 8 catches for 113 yards. Afterward he said watching himself on camera coming out of the ambulance felt like "WWE Smackdown vs. Raw." {{< x url="https://x.com/BleacherReport/status/2115992815054577734" label="Christian Watson is back" >}} (~260K views). - **Sunday's highlights:** Vikings at Lions (Minnesota is the NFL's last unbeaten team), Chiefs at Broncos for first place in the AFC West at 4:25 PM ET on CBS, and Eagles at Commanders at 8:20 PM ET on NBC. Monday night is Bears at Seahawks.

**NBA**

- **Grizzlies 124, Trail Blazers 103.** Rookie Cameron Boozer had 18 points, 12 rebounds and 12 assists. At 19 years, 103 days old, he's the second-youngest player ever with a triple-double, behind only Josh Giddey. Memphis is 5-0 for the first time since 2014-15, and Portland is 3-1. Ja Morant scored 14 in his first game against his old team but left in the 4th quarter with a hamstring injury. {{< x url="https://x.com/NBA/status/2116031130801524834" label="Cam Boozer triple-double, Grizzlies stay undefeated" >}} (~255K views). It's the NBA's top clip of the night. Boozer is also the youngest player to post a triple-double in a win.
- **Hawks 114, Cavaliers 111.** Onyeka Okongwu had a season-high 29 points and 12 rebounds. Nickeil Alexander-Walker added 25 and hit two free throws with 15 seconds left, then Donovan Mitchell missed two tying looks at the buzzer. James Harden led Cleveland with 24, and Mitchell went 1-for-9 from three. Atlanta (3-3) snapped a three-game skid, and Cleveland (3-2) saw its three-game win streak end.
- **Pelicans 132, Kings 120.** Jeremiah Fears had 22 points, a career-high 13 assists, 8 rebounds and 4 blocks. Zion Williamson scored 22 on 10-of-12 shooting. It's New Orleans' first win (1-4) and its first under coach Jamahl Mosley. The Pelicans forced 25 turnovers and set a franchise record with 40 points off turnovers. Rookie Darius Acuff Jr. scored a career-high 34 with 14 assists for Sacramento, which is 0-5.
