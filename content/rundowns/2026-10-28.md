**MLB: World Series Game 4**

- **Rays 4, Brewers 3 (12 inn.).** Tampa Bay avoided the sweep, and Milwaukee leads the series 3-1. Rookie Cooper Pratt hit a go-ahead solo homer in the top of the 12th. In the bottom half, Joey Ortiz's throwing error put Junior Caminero on, Jonny DeLuca's single tied it, and pinch-hitter Ben Williamson's chopper into right won it. {{< x url="https://x.com/MLB/status/2115299962787418555" label="Ben Williamson walk-off, Rays survive" >}} (~1.2M views). It's the most-watched clip of the night. It came 15 years to the day after David Freese's walk-off, and the only other team to walk off while facing a World Series sweep was the 1910 Cubs.
- Aaron Ashby extended his record scoreless relief streak to 18 innings. Milwaukee's 8-game postseason win streak is over.
- **Game 5:** tonight at 8:00 PM ET on FOX at Tropicana Field. Jacob Misiorowski starts for Milwaukee. The Rays' starter is TBA after Nick Martinez threw two innings in relief. Games 6 and 7, if needed, are in Milwaukee.
