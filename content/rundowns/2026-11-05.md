**NBA**

- **Warriors: idle Wednesday; tonight they host the Heat at Chase Center (10 PM ET, NBA TV).** Golden State (5-3) brings a five-game win streak into the game, and Steve Kerr gave Curry the highest praise there is on Wednesday: "Honestly, it really does remind me of what it was like to play with Michael Jordan back in the day," he said, pointing to Curry's longevity as much as his shooting. At 38, in his 18th season, Curry is averaging 30.0 points while shooting 42.9% from three, and Monday's 34 against Phoenix made him the fourth player 38 or older with three straight 30-point games, joining Michael Jordan, Karl Malone and LeBron James. Tonight also brings Klay Thompson back to Chase Center in a Heat uniform, less than 24 hours after he won a game in overtime (see below).
