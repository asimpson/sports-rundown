**College Football (Big Ten)**

- **Michigan 18, No. 4 UCLA 16 (3OT).** The Wolverines (6-5, 3-5) ended UCLA's unbeaten season and clinched bowl eligibility on Senior Day. Michigan led 10-3 late, UCLA tied it at the end of regulation, and after the teams matched 13-13 and 16-16 in the first two overtimes, Bryce Underwood (first start since Oct. 17) hit Jalen Hoffman on the winning two-point conversion after UCLA's try failed. Michigan won the turnover battle 3-1, and fans rushed the field. Next up: The Game at Ohio State on Nov. 28. {{< youtube url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=buAAPGNtx14" >}}
