**MLB: World Series Game 3**

- **Brewers 10, Rays 1.** Milwaukee leads the series 3-0 and can win its first World Series tonight. The game turned in the 5th: eight runs, all with two outs, on seven straight hits. No team had ever strung together seven straight hits or scored eight two-out runs in a World Series inning. Robert Gasser threw six scoreless innings, and Jackson Chourio went 4-for-5. Brandon Woodruff got the final out in his first appearance since July 4. The Brewers have won 8 straight postseason games, which ties the record. {{< x url="https://x.com/MLBONFOX/status/2114917975723507894" label="Woodruff closes it out" >}} (~468K views). It's the most-watched baseball clip of the night: Woodruff's emotional return on the mound puts Milwaukee one win from a title.
- Tampa Bay's highlight was Chandler Simpson crashing into the wall in the 2nd to rob Sal Frelick of extra bases.
- **Game 4:** tonight at 8:00 PM ET on FOX at Tropicana Field. Griffin Jax starts for the Rays, and Milwaukee's starter is TBD.

**NFL: Monday Night Football**

- **Cowboys 38, Eagles 21.** Dak Prescott threw for 212 yards and 3 TDs, two of them to CeeDee Lamb. Javonte Williams ran for a career-high 155 yards, the most by a Cowboys back since Zeke in 2016, and Dallas finished with 204 rushing yards. DeMarvion Overshown's 4th-quarter interception of Tanner McKee ended Philly's last real chance. Dallas is 3-4. The Eagles are 2-5, with five straight losses, their longest skid since 2016. {{< x url="https://x.com/NFL/status/2114917676921548897" label="Javonte Williams puts Dallas up three scores" >}} (~432K views). Williams waved goodbye to the Philly crowd on the way in, and it's the top NFL clip of the night.

**NBA**

- **Warriors 134, Nuggets 114.** It's Golden State's first win (1-3). Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg had a career-high 31 points, 12 rebounds and 6 assists, and Steph Curry scored 24 on 8-of-8 shooting. {{< x url="https://x.com/NBA/status/2114942351953273245" label="Yaxel goes off for 31-12-6" >}} (~551K views). It's the most-viewed NBA clip of the night. Lendeborg is the first Warriors rookie since Curry in 2010 to post 30-10-5. It's also the first time in Curry's career he scored 20+ without missing a shot.
- **Cavaliers 127, Timberwolves 125 (OT).** Anthony Edwards scored 47, a season high, but Donovan Mitchell had 26, with the go-ahead fadeaway at 0:48 of OT. Harden added 19 and 9 assists, and rookie Meleek Thomas scored 16 in his NBA debut. Ant missed an open three at the end that would have won it.

Williams has a broken left fibula and dislocated ankle and needs surgery. There's no timetable, and he could miss the season. Cason Wallace (torn labrum) also needs surgery.
- **Hornets 110, Wizards 103.** Brandon Miller scored a career-high 39, with 27 of them in the middle quarters. Anthony Davis had 29 for Washington.
- **Mavericks 113, Heat 109.** Cooper Flagg had 29 points, 11 assists, 5 steals and 3 blocks. He hit the clinching drive with 10 seconds left and blocked Andrew Wiggins' go-ahead three. Giannis had a 29-point triple-double, but Miami is 0-4.
- **Celtics 134, Bulls 99.** Payton Pritchard scored 28, including 19 in the 3rd quarter, and went 7-of-10 from three. Tatum had 23.
- **Bucks 110, Pacers 102.** Tyler Herro had 23 points, 10 rebounds and 7 assists, and it's Milwaukee's first win.
- **Rockets 120, Hawks 110.** Alperen Şengün had 29, 9 rebounds and 9 assists, and Durant scored 22. Houston is 4-0.
- **Grizzlies 110, Jazz 105.** Memphis is 4-0. Cedric Coward had 16 points and 11 rebounds.
