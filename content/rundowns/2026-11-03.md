**NFL: Week 8**

- **Bears 34, Seahawks 17 (MNF).** Chicago led wire to wire in Seattle and beat the defending Super Bowl champs, a week after beating the AFC champs at home. NFL Research says they're the first team ever to beat both of the previous Super Bowl teams in back-to-back prime-time games. Caleb Williams threw for 247 yards, D'Andre Swift ran in three touchdowns, and newly acquired Jeffery Simmons had a sack in his Bears debut. The Bears also blocked a field goal early in the fourth, then answered after Seattle cut it to 20-17. Chicago is 6-2, Seattle 4-4. He has 109 points over his last three games; at 38 or older, only Michael Jordan and LeBron James have scored that many in a three-game span. - **Pelicans 135, Grizzlies 134 (OT).** Zion Williamson scored New Orleans' first nine points of overtime, and Jeremiah Fears hit the winning free throws with 7.3 seconds left. Ty Jerome scored 40 for Memphis, including the last nine of regulation, but missed at the buzzer. Memphis was the league's last unbeaten team (6-1).

- **Raptors 125, Clippers 118.** Toronto scored 47 in the fourth quarter to erase a 16-point deficit, tying a franchise record. Scottie Barnes scored 15 in the fourth and Immanuel Quickley 14. The Clippers are 1-6.
