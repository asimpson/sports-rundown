**College Football: Big Ten**

- **No. 18 Oregon 31, Michigan 7.** The Wolverines (5-5, 2-5) were down 14-7 at the half and then turned it over on all five second-half drives (three on downs, two fumbles) at Autzen Stadium. Dylan Raiola threw for 341 yards and four touchdowns, and Oregon (7-3, 5-2) piled up 467 yards. Michigan's only points came on freshman Savion Hiter's 70-yard touchdown run, the longest by a Michigan freshman since Tyrone Wheatley in 1991; Hiter finished with 107 yards on 13 carries. Tommy Carr started and Bryce Underwood played the rest of the first half. Michigan is now 0-2 at Autzen. Next up: Saturday, Nov. 21 vs. No. 4 UCLA at 3:30 PM ET on CBS.
- **No. 6 Indiana 27, No. 15 USC 13.** Grant Wilson threw two touchdown passes in his first start and the Hoosiers (9-1, 6-1) picked off USC twice late to hold on.
- **No. 21 Ohio State 28, Northwestern 19.** Luke Fahey threw three touchdown passes, Jeremiah Smith had 11 catches for 117 yards and a score, and Bo Jackson ran for 114 as the Buckeyes (7-3, 5-2) snapped a two-game skid.
- **Penn State 42, Minnesota 14.** Rocco Becht threw three touchdown passes and ran for another, and Carson Hansen ran for 126 yards as the Nittany Lions (6-4, 3-4) became bowl eligible.
- **No. 16 Iowa 28, Purdue 7.** Hank Brown threw for a score and ran for another and LJ Phillips rushed for 123 yards as the Hawkeyes (8-2, 5-2) won their third in a row.
- **Nebraska 31, Rutgers 0.** Anthony Colandrea threw two touchdown passes and ran for another as the Cornhuskers (6-4, 3-4) kept their postseason hopes alive.
- **Maryland 24, Wisconsin 21.** The Terrapins (4-6, 2-5) beat the Badgers (6-4, 4-3) to all but end Wisconsin's Big Ten title hopes.
- **Washington 21, Michigan State 14.** Jayden Limar scored on a 1-yard run with 2:24 left to clinch a bowl bid for the Huskies (6-4, 3-4) after the Spartans (3-7, 1-6) mounted a comeback attempt.
- Friday night: No. 4 UCLA 22, Illinois 14 kept the Bruins unbeaten at 10-0.
