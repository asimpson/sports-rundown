**NBA**

- **Rockets 115, Warriors 108.** Golden State's eight-game win streak ended in Houston, and the bigger news is Stephen Curry's injury. Curry hit a 28-foot three with 7:22 left in the first quarter, yelled in pain, grabbed at his right side and left for the locker room. He was ruled out with right abdominal soreness after 6 minutes: 3 points on 1-of-3 shooting (1-of-2 from three), no other stats. He left the arena in the second half for an MRI on his abdomen/oblique, and Steve Kerr said, "Concern is high, but I have no idea the extent." Curry told teammates at halftime he'd be all right. Yaxel Lendeborg led Golden State with 25 points and 10 rebounds, Brandin Podziemski had 22, and Draymond Green had 7 points and 7 assists. Kevin Durant scored 20 for Houston (passing Shaq for seventh on the all-time field goals list), Alperen Sengun had 19 points, 13 rebounds and 9 assists, Amen Thompson 19, and Fred VanVleet 18 with four threes.
